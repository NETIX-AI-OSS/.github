## About NETIX.AI

NETIX.AI builds software for building monitoring, facility management and device integration. Operators use it to collect telemetry, diagnose equipment faults and manage maintenance.

This organization contains the public SDKs, integrations, Kubernetes utilities and device examples used by the NETIX platform.

## Platform components

These repositories cover:

- **Connected operations**, secure services, APIs, and integrations for distributed systems
- **Observability and control**, dashboards, monitoring, and operational insight
- **Platform engineering**, Kubernetes utilities and backend tooling for scalable deployments
- **Edge and device integration**, practical examples for interfacing with operational technology

They support integrations with NETIX's commercial building operations platform.
